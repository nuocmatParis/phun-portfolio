# Interaction Patterns

- Navigation and button hovers transition colors over 150ms.
- Surface-card hover changes the border to 80% border color and the background to 10% muted color.
- Social controls change to 50% muted background; their SVG stroke changes from muted foreground to foreground.
- Skill pills change from 20% muted background/muted text to full muted background/foreground text.
- The skills track moves continuously for 25 seconds per cycle and pauses while the track is hovered.
- The header adds its scrolled treatment as soon as `window.scrollY > 0`.
- The theme toggle switches the root `.dark` class and persists `dark` or `light` in local storage.
- There are no entrance reveals, modals, tabs, accordions, parallax effects, or carousels.
